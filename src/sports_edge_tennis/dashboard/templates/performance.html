Historical model validation

These are historical validation figures, not the live betting record.

They measure how each model scored against a fixed historical evidence set. They are not recomputed when the production system changes, they are not tied to the system currently generating picks, and they say nothing about money won or lost.

Accuracy is how often a model picked the eventual winner. Calibration asks whether the probabilities behave like probabilities: a 70% forecast should win about 70% of the time over a large sample.

Model Lab

See how each Tennis model performs, how reliable its probabilities are, and how much evidence sits behind the result.

Accuracy

How often the model picked the eventual winner. Useful, but not enough by itself to prove betting profitability.

Calibration

Whether probabilities behave like probabilities. A 70% forecast should win about 70% of the time over a large sample.

Forward Market Evidence

CLV and future paper results test whether the model is finding useful prices in live conditions, not just fitting history.

What each agent is looking for

Agent 1 measures surface-adjusted player strength. Agent 2 models serve/return and point-level matchup strength. Agent 3 looks for matchup and intransitivity evidence that the first two may miss, and can abstain when evidence is too weak.

Important distinction

The table above measures prediction quality. It does not tell us whether the system makes money at available odds. Live profitability belongs on the Results page and must use point-in-time prices captured when a recommendation was published.

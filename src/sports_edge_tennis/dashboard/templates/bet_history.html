Results

This page tracks qualified value bets that were actually publishable to the client. It is intentionally separate from Model Pick accuracy, which includes model winners even when the market price was not good enough to bet.

Qualified Bet Results

One published value bet per sporting match. Hourly reruns never create extra wins or losses.

ROI

Profit relative to stake on settled recommendations. It is different from model accuracy and should be read with sample size.

Model Pick vs Bet

A winning Model Pick is not counted here unless it also passed the market/value gate and became a publishable Bet.

What this does and does not prove

Win rate tells us how often the selected player won. ROI tells us whether the recorded prices produced profit. A high win rate can still lose money at poor prices, and a lower win rate can still be profitable at good prices.

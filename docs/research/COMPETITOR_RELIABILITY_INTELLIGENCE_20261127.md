## Scientific basis
1. Franc, Prusa, Voracek (2023), Optimal Strategies for Reject Option Classifiers, JMLR 24(11):1–49, https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v24/21-0048.html. Sections 2–3, Definition 1. For fixed classifier h and loss L, conditional risk r(x)=E[L(Y,h(x))|X=x]. An uncertainty score s is proper when r(x)<r(x') implies s(x)<s(x'). It need not be a calibrated probability. Accept low risk; randomized boundary acceptance handles ties. Cost-based rejection, maximum coverage at bounded risk, and minimum risk at bounded coverage share a Bayes selection structure under the paper's assumptions. Maximum-class-probability uncertainty 1-max(p,1-p) is optimal for 0/1 loss only when true conditional probabilities are known; empirical calibration alone does not guarantee correct conditional ranking. Loss regression and SELE are candidate research directions, not implemented here. IID/population consistency does not guarantee finite chronological tennis performance.
2. Traub et al. (2024), Overcoming Common Flaws in the Evaluation of Selective Classification Systems, NeurIPS 37, https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/047c84ec50bd8ea29349b996fc64af4b-Abstract-Conference.html. Sections 2–3, Eq. 6–7. Coverage c=P(A); selective risk R(c)=P(error|A); generalized risk G(c)=P(error,A)=c R(c). AURC integrates R(c) over [0,1]; AUGRC integrates G(c) over [0,1], lower better, binary range [0,.5]. With accuracy a and failure-detection AUROC u (larger uncertainty means failure), AUGRC=(1-u)a(1-a)+(1-a)^2/2; random ranking (1-a)/2, ideal ranking (1-a)^2/2. AURC can overemphasize top-ranked failures and violate monotonicity; AUGRC complements but does not replace working-point accuracy or calibration. Brier=mean((p-y)^2); log loss=-mean(y log p+(1-y)log(1-p)); these are proper probability scores, not proper uncertainty-ranking scores. Neither paper establishes tennis-specific performance.
